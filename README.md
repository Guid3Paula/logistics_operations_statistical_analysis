## Business Problem Definition

### 1.1 Context
A logistic company handles a significant volume of deliveries every day and collects differents operacional data about its routes, including distance traveled, delivery time, number of stop, and the final operating cost.  

Despite the availability of this data, some decisions related to route planning, cost control, and operational efficiency are still based on aggregated metrics or team experience, without a deeper statistical understanding os the factors associated with operational performance.  

---

### 1.2 Business Problem
The company faces the challenge of understanding which operational characteristics differenciate high-cost routes from more efficient routes.

This lack of visibility may results in:
- **High Operating Costs:** Without a structured analysis of the data, it becomes more difficult to identify which route characteristics are associated with higher transportation costs.

- **Low Operational Efficiency:** Routes with similar distances may presents significantly different delivery times, numbers of stops, and operating costs, indicating possible opportunities for optimization.

- **Difficulty Identifying Patterns:** The absence of quantitative analysis limits the ability to identify groups of routes with specific characteristics such as high-cost operations or routes with higher operational efficiency.

- **Insufficiently Data-Driven Decisions:** Logistics planning may be performed without a clear understanding of which variables are most strongly associated with the final operating cost.

---

### 1.3 Main Objective  

Use statistical analysis of operational data to understand delivery route behavior, identify characteristics associated with higher cost, and segment operations according to different levels of efficiency.  

The analysis aims to provide insights that can support decisions related to route plainning, cost control, and operational efficiency improvement.

---

### 1.4 Key Questions to Be Answered  

The data analysis should answer the following business questions:  

- **1 - What is the average route profile in terms of distance traveled, delivery time, number of stops, and operation costs?**

- **2 - What are the main characteristics os routes classified as "High Cost"? Do they cover longer distance, include more stops, or require more delivery time?**

- **3 - Are there routes with high operational utilization, such as longer distances or a higher number of stops, but relatively low cost? What characteristics differentiate these operations?**

- **4 - Is there a statistically relevant correlation between distance traveled, delivery time, number of stops, and the final route cost?**

---

### 1.5 Expected Results and Business Impact

The results od this project will be a statistical analysis of logistics operations that enables:

- **Operational Segmentation:** Identify different groups of routes, such as "High-Cost Routes" and "Operationally Efficient Routes", enabling more targeted analysis.

- **Identification of Cost-Related Factors:** Evaluate which operational characteristics show the strongest relationship with the final route cost.
